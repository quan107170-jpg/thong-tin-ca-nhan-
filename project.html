Problem Statement

Tackling Information Scatter in High School Academics

In our AP Biology and Chemistry block, our instructor shared resources through disconnected channels. The Classroom Website Portal was conceived as a self-hosted, lightweight home base providing instant visibility into today's syllabus and upcoming deadlines.

Project Directives

  • check_circle Zero heavy external dependencies (pure HTML/CSS).
  • check_circle High-contrast color scheme compliant with WCAG 2.1 AA.
